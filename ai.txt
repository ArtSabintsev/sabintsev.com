## About
Personal homepage for Arthur Ariel Sabintsev (Washington DC–Baltimore). COO of [Modern Intelligence](https://modernintelligence.ai). The product is **Sovereign**: onboard targeting software that OEMs and integrators install on UAVs, USVs, UUVs, and other unmanned vehicles. It runs on the vehicle’s computer — detect, classify, track, keep identity when the picture breaks up, terminal guidance into impact — including GNSS-denied / EW (GPS out, radio jammed). Previously co-founded Grove (wound down 2026) and Pocket Network (trillion+ API requests; POKT $1B+ market cap). Path: experimental nuclear physics (Jefferson Lab, GW) → iOS (Fueled, Shelby.tv) → first mobile engineer at ID.me → Washington Post Arc XP Mobile → Capital One Payments → Grove / Pocket → defense AI. Writes on AI agents, stablecoins, and operator craft. Full background: [llms-full.txt](https://sabintsev.com/llms-full.txt).

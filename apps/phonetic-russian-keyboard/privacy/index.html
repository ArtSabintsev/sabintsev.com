Privacy Policy

Phonetic Russian Keyboard · Last updated: August 8, 2026

Phonetic Russian Keyboard (“the App”) is a custom iOS keyboard and companion host app published by Arthur Sabintsev.

Data collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal data or keystroke data.

  • The keyboard extension does not require Full Access.
  • The keyboard does not use the network.
  • There is no analytics, advertising, or account system.

Data use

Typed text is only inserted into the active text field on your device via the iOS text input system. We never receive that text.

Third parties

The App does not integrate third-party SDKs for tracking, advertising, or analytics.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will be revised and the new policy published at this URL.
